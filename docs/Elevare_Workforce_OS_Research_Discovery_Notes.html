01 Company & Role Context

Elevare Human Solutions Ltd is a Nigerian company whose current business helps other companies with HR needs, recruitment, workforce services. I'm the platform manager and technical lead for a new product, Elevare Workforce OS, working alongside our HR lead, who acts as product owner and holds the business vision. I have an existing background in Python, FastAPI, and Django.

Elevare Workforce OS is being built as a brand-new, standalone product, sold alongside Elevare's existing recruitment platform, not as a feature added to it. It's scoped from the start as a multi-tenant SaaS product for external paying customers, not an internal tool used by Elevare first.

02 Product Vision

Elevare Workforce OS is an Enterprise Workforce Execution Platform. It connects business strategy, critical talent, operational execution, AI, and intelligent payroll in one ecosystem. It's positioned to close a gap in traditional HR tools: they manage administration well but give leadership little visibility into whether strategy is actually being executed, or which roles/people matter most to outcomes.

Best classified as an HCM platform with a strategy-execution layer, not a full ERP, no finance, inventory, or procurement modules.

03 MVP Scope From my HR/product owner

This scope came directly from my HR lead, who owns the product vision, not from my own assumption: company onboarding and organisational setup, Business DNA configuration, department and reporting structure, employee management, OKR and KPI setup, daily task assignment linked to KPIs, attendance and clock-in, employee/manager/executive dashboards, workflow management, performance tracking, reporting and analytics, and intelligent payroll based on approved execution metrics. One addition I proposed and she accepted: critical-role vacancy flagging, a lighter version of full recruitment integration, which we agreed to defer.

This is business scope, not a technical constraint, if something here has technical implications worth questioning, I'd like to hear it.

04 AI Approach From my HR/product owner

Hybrid, AI suggests, human approves. Manual input is limited to Business DNA capture only, vision, mission, industry, value chain. Everywhere else the system can help, Critical Role suggestions, KPI suggestions, nothing auto-activates, everything requires human review (approve, edit, or reject).

For MVP this is rules and template based, not real machine learning, there's no dataset anywhere of "which roles are critical per industry" since that's proprietary business judgment nobody publishes. Every approval or correction a customer makes becomes labeled data, which is the realistic long-term path to a trained model, not something available on day one.

Business DNA questions themselves are a fixed, structured questionnaire, not dynamically AI-generated.

07 Design & Frontend Status In progress

A frontend/UI-UX developer has been hired and is actively working from the real PRD, not a test brief. As of this handover, roughly 28 screens have been designed in Figma. Strong coverage exists for auth/onboarding, all three dashboards, employee management, the AI suggestion/approval pattern (Critical Roles specifically), task assignment, and payroll.

Known gaps as of this handover

  • KPI Suggestions screen, the AI-suggestion pattern exists for Critical Roles but not yet for KPIs, same pattern needs replicating
  • Workflow Management screen, referenced in scope but not yet designed
  • Settings page, several tabs (Roles & Permissions, Integrations, Security, Audit Log) exist as navigation only, with no defined content behind them yet, this needs scoping before it's built further
  • One known rendering bug on the Employee Details page, overlapping text

08 Infrastructure Decisions

  • Development hosting: Railway (or similar), chosen deliberately for low cost during dev, since there are no real users yet
  • Production hosting: Azure, planned but not yet finalized, pending a conversation with a local Microsoft partner (CloudSA), who has also proposed handling security/identity infrastructure and potentially AI tooling via Azure OpenAI
  • Email: Resend, free tier, using a subdomain of the company domain
  • Domain: to be purchased, will serve both the app and the email subdomain
  • Dev tooling: AI-assisted development tooling (Claude/ChatGPT API) requested as productivity tooling given team size and timeline

09 Development Plan Status

Development began Monday, Sep 7, 2026, following a nine-phase plan I put together myself: foundation and auth first, the strategy/AI suggestion loop given the most time since it's the core differentiator, payroll placed last since it depends on everything else being trustworthy first, hardening and launch prep at the end.

I'm committed to this timeline and working toward it, not looking to relitigate the schedule itself, but I'm very open to your input on how each phase gets built, and on the technical choices within it.

10 Things I'd Like to Go Through Together

These are the questions where I'd genuinely value your judgment, not confirmation of something I've already decided.

Is the Azure/CloudSA direction sound?

I don't have independent judgment on whether committing to Microsoft's ecosystem (hosting, identity, potentially AI) is the right call for a team our size, versus staying more vendor-neutral longer.

FastAPI or Django for the backend?

I have working experience in both. I'd like to walk through the trade-offs together, given the multi-tenancy needs, the timeline, and that I'll largely be building this alone.

Is the rules-based AI suggestion approach the right MVP call, or should RAG be pulled forward?

We deliberately scoped AI down to rules/templates for MVP, given team size. Worth a second opinion on whether that's still right given the product's positioning and the marketing push planned around launch.

Are there architectural risks I'm not seeing?

I'd specifically welcome a critical look at the multi-tenancy approach (shared schema plus Row-Level Security) and whether it will genuinely hold up once real customer data and real payroll numbers are involved.

What am I not asking that I should be?

Given this is my first time building something at this scope, I'd rather hear what's missing from this brief than assume I've covered everything.
