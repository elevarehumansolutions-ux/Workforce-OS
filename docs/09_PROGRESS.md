### M12 — Performance: Scoring, Dashboards & Executive Intelligence
**Backend, also here (2026-09-30, `08_DECISIONS.md`):** wherever a KPI's live progress is surfaced on a dashboard, a `task_count`-tracking KPI reads M10's live task-count (no stored figure to read from `kpi_scores` mid-quarter); a `manual`-tracking KPI reads whatever was last upserted via `POST /kpis/{id}/scores` for the current period. **Also in scope, added 2026-09-22 (Jennifer's escalation/accountability submission):** `employee_kpi_scores`/`kpi_scores` calculation reads `task_blocks` (M10) before scoring a task, a `pending`-review block holds that task out of the period's calculation entirely until reviewed; an `approved` block excludes its duration from lateness; a `rejected` one scores as if never blocked. `executive_summaries`' AI narrative generation also reads `task_blocks` (department, category, duration, outcome), the same shared LLM utility, a new content type, not a new integration, to distinguish systemic patterns ("Operations missed target primarily due to repeated cross-department approval delays") from genuine individual underperformance.
