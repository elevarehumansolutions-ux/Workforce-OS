## What M12 is

M1 to M11 *store* the facts. M12 turns them into **scores and dashboards**:

- **Employee scores:** `employee_kpi_scores`, one row per employee per KPI per period, worked out from that
  employee's own tasks linked to a KPI (`tasks.kpi_id`): were they done, and on time (`due_at` against
  `completed_at`), with blocks taken into account.
- **KPI and department scores:** `kpi_scores` (already exists, M8) gets its final figure; the department's
  score is its KPIs' weighted results.
- **Dashboards:** `GET /departments/{id}/performance-summary` (quarterly revenue-target rollup),
  `GET /employees/leaderboard` (ranked employee scores), `GET /company-performance-summary` (the headline
  Company Performance Score, quarterly trend plus a custom date range, Most Improved).
- **Executive summary:** `executive_summaries`, a short LLM-written narrative of a period ("Operations
  exceeded target by 12%..."), generated by a Celery Beat job, stored, never generated per page view. It also
  reads `task_blocks` (department, category, duration, outcome) so it can tell a systemic delay from an
  individual one.
- **The quarter-end close job for `task_count` KPIs** (deferred from M10): at the end of a fiscal quarter, count
  the completed linked tasks one last time and write the figure into `kpi_scores`, the same upsert
  `POST /kpis/{id}/scores` uses, triggered by the scheduler instead of a person.

This is the investor-facing part of the product (`08_DECISIONS.md` 2026-09-07): treat polish and sensible empty
states as first-class.

## What earlier milestones already give you (don't rebuild)

- **KPIs (M8):** `kpis` with a `weight` per department or location that sums to 100, `tracking_mode`
  (`manual` or `task_count`), `is_inverse` (lower is better). `kpi_scores` is one row per KPI per period, a
  running figure while the quarter is open (upserted by `POST /kpis/{id}/scores`) and **permanent history
  once the period closes** (no `deleted_at`). `KPIService.calculate_score_percentage` already turns an actual
  and a target into a percentage, honouring `is_inverse` (None rather than a made-up number when the target
  is zero).
- **Live task count (M10):** `GET /kpis/{kpi_id}/task-progress` (in the Task module) counts completed linked
  tasks in the current fiscal quarter; `tasks/periods.py` (`current_fiscal_quarter`) and `core/fiscal.py` know
  the organization's fiscal quarters and timezone.
- **Task facts (M10):** every task has `due_at`, `completed_at`, a status (`open`, `in_progress`,
  `completed`, `cancelled`; a cancelled task counts as neither completed nor late), `kpi_id`, an assignee that
  can be empty, and block data (`blocked_since`, `approved_blocked_seconds`, and `task_blocks` rows with a
  review status of pending, approved or rejected). The overdue rule is written once in `tasks/overdue.py` and
  is block-aware. **The rules already decided for blocks in scoring** (`01_REQUIREMENTS.md` 6a): a pending
  block holds that task out of the period's calculation entirely until it is reviewed; an approved block
  excludes its duration from lateness; a rejected block scores as if it had never been blocked.
- **Workflow tasks (M11) are ordinary tasks:** created with no human creator, possibly unassigned, with a
  business-hours deadline counted from when the previous step ended. A step skipped by hand is a cancelled
  task (neither completed nor late). `tasks.workflow_instance_id` and `workflow_step_id` are available if
  scoring ever needs them. The known limitation stays: a whole workflow can run long while every step is
  individually on time; that is a process-throughput metric nobody has requested (`08_DECISIONS.md`
  2026-09-23).
- **Patterns to reuse:** a Celery Beat job that finds organizations through a narrow `SECURITY DEFINER`
  function (the quarterly objective review, the overdue scan, the offboarding handover; each new
  cross-tenant lookup is logged in `07_SECURITY.md`), the shared LLM utility `ai/llm.py`
  (`generate_structured`, model tiers, `ai_usage_log`), offset and cursor pagination helpers.
- **Rule from the docs to restate in code:** a KPI's weight can change after scores for past periods exist;
  those historical scores must stay exactly as computed, never silently recalculated.

## Design questions to settle before building (none decided yet)

1. **How is one employee's score for one KPI worked out from their tasks?** This is the central question.
   Options to put to the user with a real example: completion only; on time against late with partial credit;
   Jennifer's example (closing a lead in 5 to 7 days scores well, faster scores higher, slower scores lower).
   What is the denominator (tasks due in the period, or a target number)? Does it differ for `task_count` and
   `manual` KPIs?
2. **Which tasks count in a period?** By `due_at`, `completed_at` or creation. A task due in Q3 and finished in
   Q4; open tasks at quarter end; tasks with no `due_at`; unassigned tasks; a task reassigned part-way (who
   gets the credit: the assignee at completion?).
3. **A pending block at quarter end versus "a closed period is permanent".** A pending review holds the task out
   until reviewed, but a closed `kpi_scores` row must never change. What happens when a period closes with a
   block still pending? (A real gap, found while writing this file.)
4. **When are scores computed and stored?** Live on read, by a nightly Beat job, on each task completion, or at
   quarter close only. `employee_kpi_scores` is a stored table in the design, so what writes it and when? How
   does a mid-quarter dashboard read differ from a closed quarter?
5. **How do scores roll up?** An employee's overall score from their KPI scores (weighted? what about a KPI
   they have no tasks on: ignored or zero?); a department's score from its KPIs' weights.
6. **What is the Company Performance Score?** A plain average of departments, or weighted (for example by each
   department's revenue allocation from M7)? Which departments count (critical ones only?).
7. **Most Improved:** the biggest quarter-over-quarter change in points or in percent, and the minimum amount
   of activity needed so one task cannot make someone "most improved".
8. **Who may see what.** HR and executives (see everything) are clear; a manager (own department only? by
   reporting line, which M10 noted as an improvement to revisit here); an employee (own score only?). Is the
   leaderboard visible to everyone or only to HR, executives and managers? Ties, and a minimum number of tasks
   to be ranked.
9. **Trend and custom date range.** Scores are stored per quarter. For an arbitrary range, do we aggregate the
   stored quarters (whole quarters only) or compute from tasks live?
10. **The executive summary.** When is it generated (each recalculation, or once at quarter close), is it
    regenerated or kept once per organization per period, what facts is the LLM given and how do we stop it
    inventing numbers, which model tier, cost limits, retry and failure behaviour, who may read it (HR and
    executive?), and what it says when there is almost no data.
11. **Empty and demo states.** What each dashboard endpoint returns for an organization with no scores yet
    (the handoff flagged empty dashboards for investor demos; `08_DECISIONS.md` 2026-10-04: no seed script).
